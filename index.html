Taha HQ

A personal assistant tool used by one person, its owner, Taha Ghula. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

Once an hour, Taha HQ reads the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail with read-only access, and turns them into short notes for a private daily briefing: upcoming events, and emails that need a reply or have a deadline.

What it can't do

  • It can't send, delete, label or change any email or calendar event. Google grants it read-only access.
  • It doesn't share anything publicly or with other people, and it shows no ads.
