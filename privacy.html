Privacy policy

Taha HQ · last updated 12 November 2026

Taha HQ is a personal tool used only by its owner, Taha Ghula. This policy explains what it does with data from Google services. The only Google account it is connected to is the owner's own.

What it reads

  • Google Calendar (read-only): events for today and the next 14 days.
  • Gmail (read-only): recent messages, to find the ones that need a reply, have a deadline or otherwise matter.
  • The account's email address, to confirm the right account is signed in.

How it uses the data

  • The data is read on the owner's own computer. An AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) sorts the emails into short summaries. It runs with no tools, so it can't act on anything an email says.
  • Only short summaries and event details are kept, in the owner's private notes, which sync only to the owner's own devices and private server. The full text of emails is not stored.
  • The summaries are used only to prepare the owner's own daily briefing.

What it never does

  • It never sends, deletes, labels or changes emails or calendar events.
  • It never sells data, shares it with anyone else, uses it for advertising, or uses it to train AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads the data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Taha HQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Email summaries cover the last 30 days and older ones drop off. Calendar details cover the next 14 days. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security, then "Third-party apps with account access". The tool can no longer read anything after that.
